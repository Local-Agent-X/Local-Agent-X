# Kids deck playbook

**Audience:** children, often early readers, watching a screen together with an adult leading.

**Story, in order:** a cover that names the topic in a few words with a big picture → one idea per slide: a big picture (`image`, or `content` with an image) and one short sentence → a question to the class every few slides (`statement`: "What do you think happens next?") → a counting or ordering moment (`stats` with small numbers they can count, `timeline` for first / next / last) → a recap of the two or three things they learned → a cheerful ending (`closing`).

**Text:** at most 20 words and 3 bullets a slide, short everyday words, type at 24pt or larger. Write sentences an adult can read aloud exactly as written.

**Design:** sunny, or studio for older kids.

**Imagery:** bright, friendly, clearly drawn pictures of the actual subject. Nothing frightening, nothing with small text inside it.

**Leave out:** lists, jargon, dense charts, anything an adult would have to explain away.

**Review the rendered deck:** Could a six-year-old say what each slide is about from the picture alone? Is any text too small, or too long to read aloud in one breath?
