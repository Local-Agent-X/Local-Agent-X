# Legal and compliance deck playbook

**Audience:** people who will rely on the exact wording: counsel, compliance officers, boards, regulators, staff bound by a policy. Accuracy and restraint over persuasion.

**Story, in order:** cover with the matter, the date and the audience → scope (what this covers and what it does not) → numbered sections (`section` dividers: "1. Obligations", "2. …") → each requirement on its own slide with its source (statute, clause, policy number) → obligations and owners (`columns`: requirement / who / by when) → deadlines (`timeline`) → summary and contacts (`closing`), with a note that the deck is not legal advice when it is not.

**Text:** quote operative language exactly and cite it; never paraphrase a requirement into something stronger or weaker. Defined terms capitalized and used consistently. Up to about 130 words and 8 bullets a slide.

**Design:** ledger or graphite only. Color never carries a legal distinction on its own.

**Imagery:** none, or neutral diagrams. No stock photos (gavels, handshakes, scales).

**Leave out:** marketing claims, predictions of outcomes, anything not supported by a cited source.

**Review the rendered deck:** Is every requirement cited? Is quoted wording exact? Is it clear what is in and out of scope? Is there any sentence that says more than its source does?
