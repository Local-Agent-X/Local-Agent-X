# Promo and event deck playbook

**Audience:** people deciding at a glance whether to care: on a screen in a store or lobby, in a social post, or opening an event.

**Story, in order (5–8 slides):** a cover that is mostly picture, with the headline → the offer or the news (`statement`, one line) → what it is (`image` full bleed with a caption) → the details that matter (`stats`: the date, the price, the number) → why it is worth it (`columns` of three short reasons, or a `quote`) → the call to action (`closing`: what to do, by when, where: website, address, code).

**Text:** headlines of a few words, at most 30 words and 4 bullets a slide, nothing under 20pt. Date, time, place and price written out in full; never "soon" or "TBD" on a finished slide.

**Design:** studio, ember, signal, evergreen or ivory to match the subject (evergreen for health and food, ivory for hospitality and retail); the business's brand whenever there is one.

**Imagery:** big, real photos of the product, venue or people; one strong image per slide.

**Leave out:** paragraphs, fine print (put terms in speaker notes or your reply), more than one call to action.

**Review the rendered deck:** From across a room, can you read the headline, the date and what to do next? Is there exactly one call to action?
