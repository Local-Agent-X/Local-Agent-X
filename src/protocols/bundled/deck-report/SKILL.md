# Report deck playbook

**Audience:** someone reading alone, with no presenter to explain. Each slide must stand on its own.

**Story, in order:** cover → executive summary (bullets: the three findings and the recommendation) → one section per finding (`section`), each with its evidence (`content` with a chart, `stats`, `columns` for comparisons) → recommendation and next steps (`closing`) → sources and method (a last `content` slide).

**Text:** titles are full-sentence findings ("Churn doubled after the price change"). Body text in full sentences; up to about 170 words and 8 bullets a slide is fine when it is read, not projected. Every number carries its source and date on the slide.

**Design:** graphite, midnight or ivory; the author's brand if there is one.

**Imagery:** charts over photos; a chart's title says what it shows.

**Leave out:** presenter cues ("as I mentioned"), decoration that adds no information.

**Review the rendered deck:** Would a reader who skips to any slide understand it? Does every number have a source? Does the summary match the evidence slides?
