# Sales deck playbook

**Audience:** a buyer asking "does this solve my problem, is it safe to choose, what does it cost". Their problem is the subject, not your company.

**Story, in order (8–12 slides):** cover with the customer's name in the tagline → their situation and the cost of the problem (`statement` or `stats`) → what changes with you (`content` with an image of it working) → how it works (`timeline` of 3–5 steps) → proof (`quote` from a real customer, `stats` of results) → options and pricing (`columns`, the recommended one in the middle) → next step (`closing`: one action, a date, a name).

**Text:** speak in the customer's words and outcomes ("hours back each week"), not features. At most 50 words and 5 bullets a slide.

**Design:** ember, harbor or midnight; the seller's brand when there is one, and never the customer's logo without permission.

**Imagery:** the product in use, or the customer's world. Screenshots over stock.

**Leave out:** claims without proof, competitor bashing, more than three options.

**Review the rendered deck:** Would this customer see their problem in the first three slides? Is every claim backed by a quote, a number or a demo? Is the next step a single, dated action?
