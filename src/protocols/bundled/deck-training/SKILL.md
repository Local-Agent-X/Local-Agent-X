# Training deck playbook

**Audience:** learners who must be able to do something afterwards. They need clarity and repetition more than persuasion.

**Story, in order:** cover with what they will learn → objectives (`columns` or 3 bullets: "By the end you can…") → one section per skill (`section` divider) → each concept on its own slide: explain it (`content` with a picture or screenshot) → show the steps (`timeline`) → common mistakes (`columns`: do / don't) → recap (`statement` or bullets restating the objectives) → where to get help (`closing`).

**Text:** plain words, define every term once, the same name for the same thing throughout. At most 55 words and 5 bullets a slide; type no smaller than 18pt so the back row can read it.

**Design:** harbor, evergreen or sunny (sunny for younger or informal groups); the organization's brand if there is one.

**Imagery:** real screenshots and photos of the actual tool, place or equipment, explained in the caption.

**Leave out:** anything the learner does not need to do the task; put background in speaker notes.

**Review the rendered deck:** Could someone follow the steps from the slides alone? Does each concept get its own slide? Does the recap match the objectives?
