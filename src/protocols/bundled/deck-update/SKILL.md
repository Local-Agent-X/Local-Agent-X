# Update deck playbook

**Audience:** people who know the context and want to know what changed, whether it is on track, and what they must decide.

**Story, in order (5–10 slides):** cover with the period ("Q3 update") → the headline in one sentence (`statement`: on track, ahead, or behind and why) → key numbers (`stats`, with the change against last period in the label) → progress (`timeline` or `columns`: done / in progress / next) → risks and blockers (`columns` or bullets, each with an owner) → decisions needed (`closing`, each as a yes/no question).

**Text:** titles that state the finding ("Revenue up 12%, churn flat"). At most 70 words and 6 bullets a slide. Dates and owners beat adjectives.

**Design:** graphite, harbor or midnight; the company's brand if there is one. Restrained: no decorative photos.

**Imagery:** charts and stats, not stock photos.

**Leave out:** history the audience already knows, vanity numbers, problems without an owner.

**Review the rendered deck:** Can someone who reads only the titles tell the status? Is every risk owned? Is every decision phrased so it can be answered in the meeting?
